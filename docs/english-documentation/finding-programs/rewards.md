# Rewards & Reputation

On DevSolve, your research excellence is recognized both monetarily through direct financial bounty payouts and professionally through global platform reputation.

***

## 🏆 Reputation Points System

Reputation points reflect your validated track record, technical accuracy, and adherence to professional submission standards:

| Report Action or Final Status           | Points Awarded / Deducted                      |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Critical Report** Resolved            | **+50 Points**                                 |
| **High Report** Resolved                | **+30 Points**                                 |
| **Medium Report** Resolved              | **+15 Points**                                 |
| **Low Report** Resolved                 | **+5 Points**                                  |
| **Duplicate Report** (First occurrence) | **0 Points** (Neutral)                         |
| **Informative Report**                  | **+1 Point**                                   |
| **Spam / Malicious / Fake Report**      | **-15 Points** (May trigger account sanctions) |

* **Reputation Privileges:** Higher reputation unlocks exclusive invitations to **Private Bounty Programs**, fast-track triage queues, and visibility on the global leaderboard.

***

## 💵 Bounty Payouts & Balance Management

Once a company awards a monetary bounty, the funds immediately reflect in your platform wallet balance:

1. **Balance Overview:** Visit [**Rewards & Balance**](https://devsolve-frontend.vercel.app/dashboard/rewards/) to inspect historical payouts, pending settlements, and downloadable tax invoices.
2. **Supported Withdrawal Channels:**
   * **Direct Wire / Local Bank Transfer**
   * **Stripe / PayPal / Payoneer**
   * **Cryptocurrency (USDT / USDC)** (availability subject to regional compliance)
3. **Settlement Timelines:** Payout withdrawal requests are reviewed and disbursed within **2 to 5 business days** following successful identity verification (KYC/AML).
