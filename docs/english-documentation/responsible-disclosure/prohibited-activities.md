# Prohibited Activities

DevSolve enforces zero-tolerance measures against activities that compromise user privacy, degrade platform reliability, or cause harm to participating organizations.

***

## 🚫 Strictly Forbidden Actions

1. **Denial of Service (DoS / DDoS):**
   * Volumetric traffic floods, application-layer exhaustion attacks, or stress tests that degrade system responsiveness for real customers.
2. **Social Engineering & Phishing:**
   * Spear-phishing, vishing, or deceptive manipulation aimed at tricking corporate staff or customers into surrendering credentials.
3. **Destructive Data Alteration:**
   * Deleting production records, modifying database schemas, altering customer account balances, or defacing customer-facing assets.
4. **Mass Data Exfiltration:**
   * Demonstrating an injection flaw requires only minimal evidence (e.g., retrieving `current_user()` or querying a single sample row). Dumping entire production databases constitutes criminal exfiltration.
5. **Extortion & External Coercion:**
   * Demanding monetary payment outside the DevSolve platform or threatening to disclose proprietary customer data.

***

## ⚖️ Sanctions for Violations

Researchers found engaging in prohibited activities face immediate punitive action:

* Permanent account banning and immediate forfeiture of accrued wallet balances.
* Complete revocation of Safe Harbor legal protections.
* Escalation and referral to law enforcement agencies and cybercrime authorities.
