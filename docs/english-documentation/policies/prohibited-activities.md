# Prohibited activities

Unless a Program explicitly authorizes an activity, do not perform or publish:

- Denial-of-service, destructive, or availability-impacting tests
- Social engineering, phishing, impersonation, or physical intrusion
- Access beyond the minimum needed to demonstrate impact
- Data destruction, alteration, persistence, or exfiltration
- Testing against out-of-scope assets or third parties
- Credential attacks or automated scanning prohibited by Program rules
- Exposure of secrets, tokens, personal data, or private Reports
- Malware, harmful payloads, harassment, spam, plagiarism, or illegal content
- Attempts to manipulate votes, reputation, rewards, or moderation

{% hint style="danger" %}
Stop testing if an action could harm users or systems. Preserve only minimal sanitized evidence and notify the organization through its authorized private channel.
{% endhint %}

DevSolve may restrict content or accounts through supported moderation and security processes. Exact consequences depend on evidence, platform policy, Program terms, and applicable law.
