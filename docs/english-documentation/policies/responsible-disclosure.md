# Responsible disclosure

<a href="https://docs.devsolve.app/km/policies/responsible-disclosure" class="button secondary">🇰🇭 ខ្មែរ</a>

Responsible disclosure keeps sensitive findings private while the affected organization validates and addresses them.

## Researcher responsibilities

- Test only listed in-scope assets and permitted techniques.
- Minimize access and stop when you have enough evidence.
- Do not retain, expose, or alter real-user data.
- Submit through the private Report workflow.
- Give the organization reasonable time under its published policy.
- Keep communication accurate and professional.

## Organization responsibilities

- Publish clear Scope, exclusions, rules, and contact expectations.
- Acknowledge and triage Reports fairly.
- Protect researcher identity and submitted evidence.
- Communicate status and requests through the private workflow.
- Honor published reward and recognition commitments.

Public disclosure is allowed only when the Program policy and applicable agreement permit it. A Community Problem or Showcase is not a substitute for coordinated disclosure.
