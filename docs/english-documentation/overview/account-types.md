# Account Types

DevSolve accommodates three distinct user roles tailored to their specific operational goals within the platform ecosystem:

***

## 🧑‍💻 1. Security Researcher / Developer

Designed for ethical hackers, penetration testers, independent security researchers, and developers who seek to audit systems responsibly.

**Key Capabilities:**

* Explore and filter active Public and Private Bug Bounty programs.
* Submit detailed vulnerability reports with structured PoC code and steps.
* Track real-time triage updates and collaborate with company triagers via report discussion threads.
* Earn cash bounties directly to their balance and gain global Reputation Points.
* Engage with the security community via Showcases, Discussion Forums, and Leaderboard rankings.

***

## 🏢 2. Company / Organization

Designed for businesses, tech startups, fintechs, and enterprises aiming to fortify their products against digital threats.

**Key Capabilities:**

* Apply for verified organization status (Verified Organization Badge).
* Create and publish custom Bug Bounty programs with fine-grained scope declarations.
* Access a unified Triage Inbox to inspect, prioritize, and manage inbound vulnerability submissions.
* Transition report states (`Triaged`, `Resolved`, `Duplicate`, `Informative`, `Out of Scope`).
* Award bounty payouts and discretionary performance bonuses.
* Invite security engineers and team members with granular permission levels.

***

## 🛡️ 3. Platform Administrator

Internal platform operators tasked with preserving platform integrity, security, and quality control.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Review and approve company verification applications.
* Audit and approve new Bug Bounty programs before public rollout.
* Mediate disagreements and disputes between researchers and companies regarding severity ratings or report rejections.
* Moderate community discussions, showcases, and report activities to enforce platform guidelines.
