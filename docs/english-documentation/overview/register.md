# Account Registration

Getting started on DevSolve is quick and straightforward. Choose your role and follow the registration guidelines below:

***

## 🧑‍💻 Registering as a Security Researcher / Developer

1. Visit the DevSolve homepage and click **Sign Up** or navigate to the [Account Type Selection](https://devsolve-frontend.vercel.app/account-type) page.
2. Select **"Individual / Security Researcher"**.
3. Provide your account credentials:
   * **Username:** Your unique platform handle (publicly displayed across Leaderboard rankings and community posts).
   * **Full Name:** Your real legal name for identity validation and bounty compliance.
   * **Email Address:** Your active email address for critical platform notifications.
   * **Password:** A strong, unique password (minimum 8 characters with lowercase, uppercase, numbers, and symbols).
4. Review and accept the **Terms of Service** and **Responsible Disclosure Policy**.
5. Click **Create Account**.
6. Check your email inbox and click the confirmation link to activate your researcher profile.

***

## 🏢 Registering as a Company / Organization

1. Navigate to the [Account Type Selection](https://devsolve-frontend.vercel.app/account-type) screen and choose **"Company / Organization"** (or go to `/register/company`).
2. Fill out your corporate credentials:
   * **Company Name:** Legal business or entity name.
   * **Company Website:** Primary official URL (e.g., `https://example.com`).
   * **Work Email:** Professional corporate domain email (generic providers like @gmail.com or @yahoo.com may face additional verification scrutiny).
   * **Industry / Sector:** Your operational vertical (Fintech, SaaS, Healthcare, E-commerce, etc.).
   * **Representative Name & Job Title:** Name and title of the primary platform administrator (e.g., Head of Security, VP of Engineering).
   * **Password:** Secure account password.
3. Click **Register Company**.
4. Confirm your work email address via the verification email sent to your inbox.
5. Log in to your organizational dashboard and submit your **Company Verification** request before launching public bounty programs.
