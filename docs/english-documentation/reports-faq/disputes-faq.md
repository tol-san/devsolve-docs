# Disputes & Resolution

Guidelines on managing unresponsive programs, severity disagreements, and mediation workflows.

***

## ❓ 1. What should I do if a company fails to respond to my report?

* **Answer:** All participating organizations commit to operational response SLAs. If an organization remains completely silent for more than **7 business days**:
  1. Click **"Request DevSolve Mediation"** within your report dashboard view.
  2. DevSolve security operations staff will directly contact the designated security lead of the organization to unblock the triage review.

***

## ❓ 2. How do I contest an unfair report rejection or severity downgrade?

* **Answer:** If an organization closes your report as Not Applicable or downgrades a Critical finding to Low without technical justification:
  1. Post a respectful comment providing a clear technical rebuttal, demonstrating realistic threat scenarios.
  2. If an agreement cannot be reached, initiate an official **Mediation Ticket**.
  3. DevSolve’s neutral senior security engineers will audit the report, independently reproduce the finding, and render a final determination on CVSS severity and validity.

***

## ❓ 3. What if a company silently patches my vulnerability without awarding a bounty?

* **Answer:** DevSolve maintains immutable timestamp records of your submission and the target asset. If you notice a patch was deployed following your submission timestamp without status updates, request an immediate mediation audit. DevSolve will investigate the remediation timeline and enforce appropriate bounty disbursements.
