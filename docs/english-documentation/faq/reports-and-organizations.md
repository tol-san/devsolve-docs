# Reports and organizations FAQ

<a href="https://docs.devsolve.app/km/faq/reports-and-organizations" class="button secondary">ខ្មែរ</a>

<details>
<summary>Why can’t I submit to a Program?</summary>

Confirm you are signed in, the Program is active, the target is in scope, and the organization has approved your Researcher Access when required.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I edit a submitted Report?</summary>

The current report review screen warns that Reports cannot be edited after submission. Verify the Draft before submitting and use private collaboration for requested clarification.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happened to Duplicate and Needs More Information statuses?</summary>

The backend preserves detailed states. The researcher list currently groups Duplicate with Rejected and Needs More Information with Triaging, while Report detail can retain the raw state.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does the Rewards page show my real balance?</summary>

Not currently. The inspected page uses demonstration records. Use recorded Report rewards and approved organization policy; do not rely on its totals or export control as a payment commitment.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why can’t I invite a team member?</summary>

The invitee must already have a DevSolve account under that email. You must be the organization owner or have the supported authority, and an existing membership or pending invitation can prevent another invite.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is Researcher Access the same as team membership?</summary>

No. Researcher Access permits an external researcher to report to the organization. Team membership grants internal workspace roles and permissions.

</details>
