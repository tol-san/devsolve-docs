# Team Management

Security is a team sport. DevSolve empowers organizations to onboard engineers, product owners, and executives with fine-grained role-based access control (RBAC).

***

## 👥 Inviting Team Members

1. Navigate to **Team Management** (`/dashboard/team-management`).
2. Click **"Invite Member"**.
3. Enter the collaborator's corporate **Email Address** and assign an operational **Role**.
4. Click **Send Invitation**. The recipient will receive an onboarding invite link.

***

## 🔑 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Permissions

| Platform Role               | Primary Capabilities & Responsibilities                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner / Org Admin**       | Unrestricted corporate access: create/update programs, manage billing deposits, disburse bounties, invite or remove team members.                                      |
| **Security Lead / Triager** | Inspect inbound submissions, converse with researchers via private comments, transition report statuses (`Triaged`, `Resolved`, `Duplicate`), and adjust CVSS ratings. |
| **Developer / Engineer**    | Inspect vulnerability technical specifics and PoCs to craft fixes; restricted from altering program policies or awarding treasury funds.                               |
| **Billing Specialist**      | Access read/write permissions strictly for account deposits, wire invoices, and financial tax reporting.                                                               |
