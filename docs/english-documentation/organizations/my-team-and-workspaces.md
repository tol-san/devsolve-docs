# Use My Team and switch workspaces

<a href="https://docs.devsolve.app/km/organizations/my-team-and-workspaces" class="button secondary">🇰🇭 ខ្មែរ</a>

**My Team** is the member-facing view of an organization you joined. It answers three questions: which organization is active, what role you hold, and which permissions you were granted.

## Open My Team

After accepting an invitation, open **Dashboard → My Team**. This sidebar item appears for an invited organization member. Organization Owners use their owner-only Organization and Team Management screens instead.

My Team shows:

- organization name, logo, slug, and current status;
- your role and join date;
- links to the company tools your permissions allow; and
- every available permission, marked as granted or not granted.

My Team does not display the full colleague roster because that roster is part of the Owner-only Team Management service.

## Permission-based shortcuts

| Granted permission | Shortcut shown |
| --- | --- |
| `VIEW_PROGRAMS` | **Programs** |
| `CREATE_PROGRAM` | **Create a program** |
| `VIEW_REPORTS` or `TRIAGE_REPORTS` | **Reports** |
| `MANAGE_RESEARCHERS` | **Researcher access** |

If an expected shortcut or sidebar item is absent, compare the permission list in My Team and ask the organization Owner to update your access.

## Switch organizations

When your account belongs to more than one organization, a **Workspace** selector appears in the Dashboard sidebar.

1. Open the **Workspace** selector.
2. Review **Your organizations**, including your role and any non-active organization status.
3. Select the organization you want to work in.
4. Confirm its name is shown as the active Workspace before changing Programs, Reports, or Researcher Access.

The selection is remembered across navigation and page reloads. Company screens use the active organization's data and permissions. The selector is hidden when you have zero or one organization because there is nothing to switch.

You can also switch from the **Also on your account** section in My Team.

## Organization states

- **Active:** granted tools can be opened.
- **Awaiting verification:** your membership is ready, but operational tools wait for approval.
- **Suspended:** Programs and Reports are closed to the whole team.
- **Not approved:** the Owner must correct and resubmit the organization.
