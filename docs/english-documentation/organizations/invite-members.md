# Invite members

<a href="https://docs.devsolve.app/km/organizations/invite-members" class="button secondary">🇰🇭 ខ្មែរ</a>

The organization Owner can invite an existing DevSolve user into the Company Workspace and choose the access they receive.

## Before inviting someone

- The recipient must already have a DevSolve account.
- Use the email address attached to that account.
- Decide which role and permissions they need.
- Confirm the active workspace is the correct organization when your account belongs to more than one.

## Send an invitation

1. Open **Dashboard → Team Management**.
2. Select **Invite member**.
3. Enter the recipient's work email.
4. Select **Manager**, **Member**, or **Viewer**.
5. Review the permissions automatically selected for that role.
6. Add or remove permissions within the role's allowed ceiling.
7. Review the summary and select **Send invitation**.

The default role is **Member**. The submit button remains unavailable until the form contains a valid email, role, and permission selection.

## After sending

DevSolve displays an **Invitation created** confirmation. The invitation appears in the recipient's **Invitations** screen, and an email may also be sent. Copy and privately share the generated link if email delivery is delayed or disabled. The confirmation shows the expiry when the service returns one; current invitations expire after seven days.

The pending person appears in Team Management with the **Invited** status. Canceling that pending row withdraws the invitation.

## Common errors

| Error | Meaning and recovery |
| --- | --- |
| No account uses the email | Ask the recipient to register, then invite them again. |
| Already a member or invitation outstanding | Check the roster before sending another invitation. |
| Organization not approved | Complete organization verification first. |
| Permission denied | Only the organization Owner can use the current Team Management workflow. |

Use least privilege: start with the minimum access needed and expand it later from [Manage the team](manage-team.md).
