# Categories, tags, weaknesses, and auto-approval

<a href="https://docs.devsolve.app/km/administration/taxonomy-and-automation" class="button secondary">ខ្មែរ</a>

Taxonomy keeps Community and Report classification searchable and consistent.

## Categories

Use **Categories** to search, filter, create, edit, activate/deactivate, and manage a supported icon. Choose a clear name, slug, scope, and sort order. Deactivating a category can affect new authoring choices.

## Tags

Use **Tags** to maintain reusable Community topics. Merge naming variations through editorial policy instead of creating near-duplicates.

## Weaknesses

Use **Weaknesses** to maintain the vulnerability-classification catalogue used by Reports. Preserve standard identifiers and clear descriptions.

## AI Auto-Approval

Use **AI Auto-Approval** to enable or disable configured automated decisions by supported target type. Automation supplements moderation; periodically audit false approvals, false rejections, and items that remain pending.
