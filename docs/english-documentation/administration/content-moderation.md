# Content moderation

<a href="/km/administration/content-moderation" class="button secondary">ខ្មែរ</a>

Content Management covers Problems, Solutions, and Showcases; Content Reports covers user-submitted flags.

## Review Community content

1. Open **Content Management** and choose the content type.
2. Filter for pending or another review state.
3. Open the complete item, including attachments and links.
4. Check relevance, readability, prohibited content, secrets, personal data, and unsafe security detail.
5. Approve/publish or reject using the controls supported for that type.

Problem moderation publishes or rejects the item. Solutions and Showcases use pending, approved, and rejected decisions; Showcase rejection requires a reason for the author. **AI Auto-Approval** may process configured targets, but administrators remain responsible for monitoring outcomes.

## Review Content Reports

Open **Content Reports**, inspect the flag and target content, then resolve or dismiss it. Use **Moderation Log** to review recorded actions and details. Do not remove content merely because you disagree with its technical conclusion; apply the published rules consistently.
