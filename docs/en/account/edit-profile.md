# Edit Profile

Your profile serves as your digital curriculum vitae on DevSolve, spotlighting your technical acumen, validated discoveries, and community standing.

---

## ✏️ Customizing Your Profile

1. Click your avatar icon in the navigation bar and select **"Profile"** (or visit `/dashboard/profile/[username]`).
2. Click **"Edit Profile"** to access your public profile settings.
3. Configure your public attributes:
   * **Avatar & Cover Media:** Upload a high-resolution professional photo or organization emblem.
   * **Display Name & Bio:** Outline your penetration testing specializations (e.g., Smart Contracts, Mobile Reverse Engineering, Cloud IAM) and industry certifications (OSCP, OSWE, CISSP).
   * **Skill Badges:** Tag your primary attack surfaces (e.g., `OAuth`, `SSRF`, `GraphQL`, `Active Directory`).
   * **External Profiles:** Link your GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, or personal security research blog.
4. Click **"Save Changes"** to publish updates instantly.
