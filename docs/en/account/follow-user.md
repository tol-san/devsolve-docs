# Following Users

DevSolve incorporates social networking primitives empowering researchers to network, follow industry thought leaders, and track peer discoveries.

---

## 🤝 How to Follow Researchers

1. Navigate to any researcher's public profile (via Leaderboard standings, Hacktivity writeups, or Discussion posts).
2. Click the **"Follow"** button.
3. Once following a researcher:
   * You receive immediate feed updates when they publish new technical showcases or public disclosures.
   * Review and audit your **Followers** and **Following** rosters anytime directly inside your personal profile view.
