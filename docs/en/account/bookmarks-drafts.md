# Bookmarks & Saved Drafts

Accelerate daily vulnerability research workflows using platform-native Bookmarks and persistent Saved Drafts.

---

## 🔖 Managing Bookmarks

* When encountering high-value programs or engaging community threads that you wish to revisit, click the **Bookmark icon**.
* Review all saved programs and discussion posts within **[My Bookmarks](/dashboard/bookmarks)** in the primary sidebar.
* Organize your testing schedules by prioritizing bookmarked targets with active scope updates.

---

## 📝 Utilizing Saved Drafts

* Crafting rigorous, reproducible PoC reports takes time. If you need to verify root causes or capture supplementary traffic dumps, click **"Save as Draft"**.
* Access **[Saved Drafts](/dashboard/saved-draft)** to:
  * Inspect all pending, unsubmitted technical drafts.
  * Resume editing with complete Markdown preview capabilities.
  * Discard false-positive investigations without penalizing reputation metrics.
  * Dispatch verified drafts to organizations once all PoC steps are confirmed.
