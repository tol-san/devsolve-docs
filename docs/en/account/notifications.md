# Notifications

Staying informed in real time guarantees you never miss critical triage updates, bounty distributions, or collaborative clarifications.

---

## 🔔 Delivery Channels

DevSolve delivers operational telemetry across two channels:
1. **In-App Real-Time Alerts:** Displayed in the bell notification drawer and archived within `/dashboard/notifications`.
2. **Email Dispatches:** Delivered directly to your registered primary email address.

---

## 📩 Monitored Notification Events

* **Report Status Changes:** Real-time updates when reports transition between `Pending`, `Triaged`, `Resolved`, or `Duplicate`.
* **Private Ticket Comments:** When a company triager or mediator requests technical clarification or PoC re-execution.
* **Bounty Allocations:** Instant receipts upon monetary bounty awards and reputation point increments.
* **Program Scope Modifications:** Immediate notices when bookmarked programs update asset scopes or increase reward grids.
* **Community Interactions:** Alerts when peers comment on your Showcases or upvote your technical discussion contributions.
