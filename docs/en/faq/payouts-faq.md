# Bounties & Payouts FAQ

Frequently asked questions regarding financial settlements, withdrawal methods, and compliance requirements.

---

## ❓ 1. How long after triage is a bounty paid out?
* **Answer:** Organizations typically disburse bounties within **7 to 14 business days** after a submission is successfully validated (`Triaged`). Upon release, funds immediately credit your platform balance.

---

## ❓ 2. What payout withdrawal methods are available?
* **Answer:** DevSolve supports diverse withdrawal rails:
  * **International & Domestic Wire Transfer (SWIFT):** 2–5 business days to clear directly into your local banking institution.
  * **Digital Payment Gateways (Stripe / PayPal / Payoneer):** Disbursed within 24 business hours.
  * **Cryptocurrency Assets (USDT / USDC):** Real-time on-chain transfers to supported Web3 wallet addresses.

---

## ❓ 3. What is the minimum withdrawal threshold?
* **Answer:** The minimum payout withdrawal limit is **$20 USD** for digital wallets and crypto, and **$50 USD** for international wire transfers.

---

## ❓ 4. Do I need to complete Identity Verification (KYC)?
* **Answer:** Yes. In adherence with global Anti-Money Laundering (AML) standards and international financial regulations, researchers must complete identity verification (submitting valid government-issued photo identification) prior to requesting their first cash withdrawal.
