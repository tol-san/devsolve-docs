# Company Registration & Verification

To establish a trusted ecosystem and safeguard ethical researchers, all organizations must complete corporate registration and pass the **Company Verification** screening before launching public bug bounty programs.

---

## 🏢 1. Company Registration Flow

1. Navigate to `/register/company` and submit your organizational profile:
   * Legal Entity / Corporate Name
   * Primary Company Domain URL
   * Corporate Business Email (generic free-mail providers are restricted)
   * Registered Office Address & Representative Contact Details
2. Click the verification link delivered to your corporate email inbox.
3. Access your newly provisioned Organization Dashboard.

---

## 🛡️ 2. Company Verification Badge Application

Obtaining the **Verified Organization Badge** builds immediate credibility among top-tier global security researchers:

1. From the company dashboard, select **"Request Company Verification"** (or visit `/dashboard/company-verification`).
2. Provide verification documentation:
   * Certificate of Good Standing or Official Business Registration Certificate.
   * Domain Ownership Validation (DNS TXT record challenge or authorized corporate email confirmation).
   * Verified identity of the security team lead or designated corporate officer.
3. DevSolve platform administrators review and audit submitted credentials within **24 to 48 hours**.
4. Upon approval, your profile receives the verified emblem, allowing immediate launch and publication of bug bounty programs.
