# Creating & Managing Programs

A well-crafted, articulate Bug Bounty program description attracts seasoned security specialists while systematically reducing noise and false-positive submissions.

---

## 🛠️ Program Creation Workflow

Open **Program Management** (`/dashboard/program-management`) and click **"Create Program"**:

1. **General Information:**
   * **Program Title:** (e.g., *DevSolve Enterprise Bug Bounty*)
   * **Tagline & Overview:** High-level summary of your security philosophy and research objectives.
   * **Branding:** Upload high-resolution logo and banner media.
2. **Access Model:**
   * **Public:** Discoverable by all verified researchers across the global network.
   * **Private:** Strictly restricted to researchers specifically invited by your security team based on reputation and skillsets.
3. **Policy & Safe Harbor Statement:**
   * Explicitly affirm adherence to DevSolve’s Safe Harbor principles and set standard response SLAs.
4. **Scope & Bounty Grids:** (Detailed in subsequent chapters).
5. Click **"Save as Draft"** or **"Submit for Review"** to dispatch the program draft for platform administrative review.

---

## 🔄 Program Lifecycle Management

* **Draft:** Editable internal workspace; invisible to external security researchers.
* **Under Review:** Platform administrators verify policy clarity and deposit funding.
* **Published / Active:** Publicly accessible and accepting live vulnerability submissions.
* **Paused:** Temporarily halts incoming report submissions (ideal when your engineering backlog is addressing complex architectural fixes).
* **Closed:** Decommissions the program permanently while maintaining historical archives.
