# Rewarding Researchers

Expeditious bounty awards and fair compensation motivate talented researchers to continue safeguarding your product roadmap over the long term.

---

## 💳 Processing Bounty Awards

1. Within any report marked `Triaged` or `Resolved`, click **"Award Bounty"**.
2. Specify the payout parameters:
   * **Bounty Value ($):** Amount reflecting your declared reward grid.
   * **Bonus Incentive ($) (Optional):** Additional merit awards for exceptional clarity or assistance with remediation.
   * **Researcher Recognition Note:** A formal message acknowledging their ethical contribution.
3. Click **"Confirm & Disburse"**:
   * Funds are deducted from your pre-funded company deposit balance and transferred into the researcher's wallet immediately.
   * Platform reputation points are automatically calculated and credited to the researcher's profile.

---

## ⏱️ Recommended Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

To optimize community engagement, adhere to standard operational response windows:
* **Initial Response SLA:** Under **48 business hours**.
* **Triage SLA:** Reproduce and validate within **5 business days**.
* **Bounty Award SLA:** Award bounties within **7 to 14 business days** after triage (do not delay bounty disbursement until production deployment if the vulnerability validity is already established).
