# Responsible Disclosure Policy

Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD) represents the ethical cornerstone of the DevSolve security ecosystem, balancing the public interest in transparency with organizational needs for remediation lead time.

---

## 🤝 Fundamental Principles

1. **Strict Confidentiality:**
   * When you discover a vulnerability, disclose it exclusively through the private DevSolve ticketing channel.
   * You may not publicly share, publish, or leak any vulnerability details or PoCs on social media (X/Twitter, LinkedIn, personal blogs) or with third parties until the organization deploys a patch.

2. **Reasonable Remediation Timeframe:**
   * Software engineering and regression testing take time. DevSolve enforces a standard **90-day remediation window** before disclosure eligibility, unless both parties mutually agree on an accelerated or extended schedule.

---

## 📢 Requesting Public Disclosure

Once a vulnerability ticket transitions to `Resolved`:
1. Either the researcher or the host organization may click **"Request Disclosure"**.
2. Disclosure Modes:
   * **Full Disclosure:** The entire report—including reproduction steps, payload scripts, and remediation verification—is published openly.
   * **Limited / Redacted Disclosure:** Only the high-level summary, weakness classification, and resolved status are shown, scrubbing sensitive endpoints and payloads.
3. A report is published to the public **Hacktivity** feed **only upon explicit mutual consent** by both parties.
