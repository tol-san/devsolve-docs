# Safe Harbor Policy

The DevSolve **Safe Harbor** framework establishes legally binding commitments from participating organizations, safeguarding ethical researchers from civil litigation and criminal prosecution when conducting good-faith security research.

---

## 🛡️ Core Safe Harbor Protections

When you conduct security research in compliance with platform policies and program scope:
1. **Authorized Access Affirmation:**
   * The participating organization agrees that your security research constitutes authorized computer access under relevant anti-hacking statutes (e.g., the U.S. Computer Fraud and Abuse Act - CFAA and international cybercrime equivalents).
2. **DMCA Anti-Circumvention Protection:**
   * Participating organizations agree not to initiate copyright infringement or anti-circumvention lawsuits (such as DMCA 1201 claims) against you for bypassing access controls during authorized research.
3. **Platform Legal Mediation:**
   * Should any third-party questions or inquiries arise, DevSolve platform legal counsel issues formal documentation confirming that your activities were executed within an authorized bug bounty program.

---

## ⚠️ Forfeiture of Safe Harbor Protections

Safe Harbor protections are **immediately invalidated** if you intentionally violate critical boundaries:
* Conducting offensive actions against **Out-of-Scope** systems.
* Accessing, retaining, or exfiltrating personally identifiable customer data (PII) or financial records beyond what is necessary for a minimal PoC.
* Executing service-disrupting attacks (Denial of Service).
* Engaging in extortion, ransom demands, or external blackmail.
