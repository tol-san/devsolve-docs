# Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct

DevSolve is committed to maintaining a welcoming, professional, and collaborative cybersecurity community.

---

## 🌟 Core Cultural Principles

1. **Professional Decorum & Respect:**
   * Treat all peers, company triagers, and administrators with professional courtesy.
   * Harassment, hate speech, derogatory insults, and discrimination will not be tolerated.
2. **Constructive Technical Discourse:**
   * Provide actionable, constructive feedback within the Discussions and Showcases hubs.
3. **Academic & Research Integrity:**
   * Plagiarism of research writeups, duplication scripts, or tooling authored by peers is strictly prohibited.
   * Creating sockpuppet accounts to artificially upvote discussion threads or manipulate reputation metrics will lead to account restrictions.

---

## 🚩 Reporting Code of Conduct Violations

If you witness abusive behavior, harassment, or unethical conduct:
* Click **"Report Content"** on the offending thread, comment, or user profile.
* DevSolve Moderation personnel will review the incident report and take appropriate action within 24 hours.
