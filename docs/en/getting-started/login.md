# Login & Account Security

Safeguarding your DevSolve account is essential. Your profile holds sensitive vulnerability details, proprietary vulnerability reports, and payout information.

---

## 🔐 Signing In to DevSolve

1. Navigate to the DevSolve portal and click **Log In**.
2. Enter your registered **Email** or **Username** alongside your **Password**.
3. If your team authenticates through enterprise Single Sign-On (Keycloak OIDC or OAuth), select your organization's login option.
4. Click **Sign In** to access your dashboard.

---

## 🔑 Password Recovery

If you cannot access your account due to a forgotten password:
1. On the login dialog, click **"Forgot Password?"**.
2. Input the email address associated with your DevSolve account.
3. A secure, time-limited password reset link will be dispatched to your inbox.
4. Open the message, follow the verification URL, and configure a resilient new password.

---

## 🛡️ Security Best Practices

* **Strong, Unique Passwords:** Use a password with 12+ characters generated via a secure password manager.
* **Avoid Credential Reuse:** Never reuse passwords utilized across personal accounts, forums, or third-party platforms.
* **Monitor Active Sessions:** Regularly inspect your active device sessions within **Settings > Security**. Immediately terminate any unverified or stale sessions.
* **Protect Session Credentials:** Never disclose authentication tokens, authorization headers, or session cookies in public repositories, forums, or shared tickets.
