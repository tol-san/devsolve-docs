- Denial-of-Service ឬ Destructive/Availability-impacting Test
- Social Engineering, Phishing, Impersonation ឬ Physical Intrusion
- Access លើសពីអ្វីដែលត្រូវការដើម្បីបង្ហាញ Impact

- Malware, Harmful Payload, Harassment, Spam, Plagiarism ឬ Illegal Content
