# Who is DevSolve for?

### Overview

DevSolve is designed to serve four main user groups, each with distinct needs and responsibilities within the platform. By bringing these groups together, DevSolve creates a collaborative ecosystem that strengthens software security and fosters knowledge sharing.

***

### User Groups

***

#### 1. Organizations / Companies

**Who They Are:** Companies that develop software products and want to improve their security posture through bug bounty programs and community collaboration.

**Their Needs:**

* Identify security vulnerabilities before attackers can exploit them
* Manage bug bounty programs efficiently
* Validate and respond to vulnerability reports
* Access verified solutions for SDLC challenges

**Key Activities on DevSolve:**

* Create and manage bug bounty programs
* Define scope, rules, and reward structures
* Review and validate vulnerability reports
* Post SDLC problems and access solutions
* Verify organization identity and manage team members

***

#### 2. Ethical Hackers

**Who They Are:** Security researchers who identify vulnerabilities in software systems and report them responsibly to organizations.

**Their Needs:**

* Discover bug bounty programs to participate in
* Submit structured vulnerability reports
* Build professional reputation and recognition
* Track report progress and status

**Key Activities on DevSolve:**

* Discover and browse bug bounty programs
* Submit vulnerability reports with detailed information
* Track report status through the validation workflow
* Build reputation through points, badges, and leaderboards
* Receive public recognition through the Hall of Thanks

***

#### 3. Software Developers

**Who They Are:** Developers working on software projects who encounter technical challenges and want to learn from the community.

**Their Needs:**

* Find solutions to SDLC challenges
* Share knowledge and best practices
* Showcase projects and build portfolio
* Learn from verified solutions

**Key Activities on DevSolve:**

* Post SDLC problems and challenges
* Submit solutions with code snippets, diagrams, or videos
* Browse and search for verified solutions
* Vote and comment on community content
* Showcase development projects

***

#### 4. Platform Administrators

**Who They Are:** Platform moderators responsible for maintaining the integrity, security, and quality of the DevSolve platform.

**Their Needs:**

* Verify companies and organizations
* Moderate community content
* Resolve disputes between users
* Ensure platform quality and fairness

**Key Activities on DevSolve:**

* Verify company registrations
* Moderate community content (problems, solutions, showcases)
* Resolve report disputes between hackers and organizations
* Oversee vulnerability report validation
* Manage platform settings and configurations

### Collaboration Across Roles

DevSolve creates value by enabling collaboration between these user groups:

* **Organizations + Ethical Hackers:** Hackers discover and report vulnerabilities; organizations validate and reward them
* **Organizations + Developers:** Organizations share SDLC challenges; developers provide solutions
* **Developers + Ethical Hackers:** Both communities share technical knowledge and learn from each other
* **Administrators + All Users:** Administrators ensure fairness, quality, and security for everyone
